SLIDE 2: MOTIVATION & PROBLEM STATEMENT (Time: 0:45 - 2:00)
- The Industry Dilemma:
  * Email Marketing: sub-3% click-through rates, severe spam-filter routing.
  * WhatsApp Marketing: >90% open rates, instant customer engagement within minutes.
- The Core Problem:
  * Sending broadcast WhatsApp blasts causes intense "Marketing Fatigue", triggering high
    opt-out rates (>15%) and brand backlash.
  * Lack of dynamic behavioral targeting and absence of anti-fatigue frequency guardrails.
- Project Goal:
  * Build an end-to-end platform that cleans raw sales data, discovers natural behavioral
    segments, and enforces automated fatigue rules before recommending campaigns.

SLIDE 3: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW (Time: 2:00 - 3:30)
- Visual Architecture:
  * Ingestion Service: Validates invoices, handles return cancellations, drops corrupt data.
  * RFM Engine: Aggregates Recency, Frequency, and Monetary spend; applies log & Z-score scaling.
  * Clustering Engine: K-Means with programmatic hyperparameter sweep (Silhouette, DBI, CHI).
  * Fatigue Guardrails: Checks explicit consent, suppression list, and rolling 30-day frequency caps.
  * Recommendation Engine: Generates explainable campaign archetypes with plain-English rationales.
  * WhatsApp Adapter: Abstracted gateway running on a safe local Mock engine with simulated badges.

SLIDE 4: MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & FEATURE ENGINEERING (Time: 3:30 - 4:45)
- RFM Metrics per customer i:
  * Recency: R_i = (t_ref - max(t_ij))
  * Frequency: F_i = count(distinct invoice_id_ij)
  * Monetary: M_i = sum(quantity * unit_price) for non-cancellation lines.
- Skewness Normalization:
  * Spend exhibits severe right-skewed Pareto distribution.
  * We apply M'_i = ln(M_i + 1) to compress extreme tails.
  * Robust Z-score scaling z = (x - mu) / sigma standardizes features to prevent Euclidean
    dominance by monetary spend during K-Means distance calculations.
