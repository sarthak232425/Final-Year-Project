PRE-DEMO ENVIRONMENT CHECKLIST (2 Minutes Before Panel)
1. Terminal 1 (Backend): Run `uvicorn backend.main:app --reload --port 8000`
2. Terminal 2 (Frontend): Run `npm run dev` in `frontend/`
3. Browser Tab 1: Open `http://localhost:5173` (React Dashboard)
4. Browser Tab 2: Open `http://localhost:8000/docs` (Swagger Interactive API Documentation)
5. Test File: `backend/data/example_transactions.csv` ready to load.

STEP 1: SYSTEM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE (1.5 Minutes)
- Action: Open dashboard homepage at `http://localhost:5173`.
- Speech:
  "Respected professors, our project solves the retention challenge in retail e-commerce.
   While email campaigns suffer from less than 3% engagement, WhatsApp has an open rate of over 90%.
   However, conversational marketing requires strict targeting and anti-fatigue controls so that
   customers do not feel spammed.
   Our platform connects three core components:
   1. Ingesting and cleaning raw retail transactional records;
   2. Performing unsupervised RFM feature clustering with mathematical validation;
   3. Generating explainable campaign recommendations governed by an automated anti-fatigue gate."

STEP 2: DATA INGESTION & DATA HYGIENE AUDIT (2 Minutes)
- Action:
  1. Click the "Data Ingestion" tab on the sidebar.
  2. Click "Load Example Dataset" (or upload `example_transactions.csv`).
  3. Point to the Ingestion Summary card and Rejection Table.
- Speech:
  "Here, our ingestion pipeline accepts raw transactional data. Notice that out of 57 submitted
   records, 52 valid transactions were retained, 2 returns were flagged as cancellations, and
   5 defective rows were safely dropped.
   In the rejection breakdown table, we show strict data hygiene: rows with missing Customer IDs,
   negative prices, negative quantities, or unparseable date strings are rejected before they can
   distort our mathematical feature space."

STEP 3: RFM FEATURE CALCULATION & CLUSTERING (2 Minutes)
- Action:
  1. Click "Run RFM & Clustering".
  2. The dashboard automatically transitions to the "RFM & Segments" tab.
  3. Scroll through the generated segment cards (Loyal High-Value, Recent Frequent, Inactive Low-Value).
  4. Scroll down to the Customer RFM Feature Vectors table.
- Speech:
  "Once the pipeline runs, the system calculates Recency, Frequency, and Monetary spend for each customer.
   Because retail spend follows a heavy right-skewed distribution, we apply a logarithmic transformation
   followed by Z-score standardization.
   Our K-Means model partitions customers into distinct cohorts. Rather than using arbitrary hardcoded
   labels, our algorithm dynamically assigns behavioral labels based on centroid percentiles:
   - Customers with low recency, high frequency, and high monetary spend are labeled 'Loyal High-Value';
   - Customers who have not ordered in hundreds of days are labeled 'Inactive Low-Value'."

STEP 4: MATHEMATICAL MODEL VALIDATION (1.5 Minutes)
- Action:
  1. Click the "Model Metrics" tab on the sidebar.
  2. Highlight the metrics: K=3, Silhouette Score = 0.4562, Davies-Bouldin Index = 0.7150.
  3. Show the K-evaluation sweep chart (K = 2 to 6).
- Speech:
  "Rather than guessing the number of clusters, our system programmatically sweeps K from 2 to 6
   with a fixed random seed (seed=42) for reproducibility.
   As shown in this chart, K=3 achieves the peak Silhouette Score of 0.4562 and a minimal Davies-Bouldin
   Index of 0.7150. In academic literature, a Silhouette score above 0.40 on real transactional data
   confirms well-separated, high-quality clusters."

STEP 5: MARKETING FATIGUE GUARDRAILS & WHATSAPP PREVIEW (2 Minutes)
- Action:
  1. Click the "Recommendations" tab on the sidebar.
  2. Point to the top counters: 15 Total Audience | 12 Approved (80%) | 3 Blocked (20%).
  3. Click "Ineligible" to show the blocked customers.
  4. Expand the Fatigue & Policy Verifications checklist on a blocked customer:
     - CUST009: Frequency cap reached (3/3 messages in 30 days).
     - CUST003: Explicit consent missing.
     - CUST008: Global suppression list match.
  5. Click back to "Eligible" and click "Preview WhatsApp Message" on customer CUST002.
  6. Show the simulated WhatsApp chat modal with the `[SIMULATED ADAPTER]` badge.
- Speech:
  "Finally, we connect machine learning to real-world marketing ethics through our Marketing Fatigue Engine.
   Even if a customer is recommended for a VIP campaign, they will be blocked if they have not given
   consent, if they are on the opt-out list, or if they have already received 3 messages in the past 30 days.
   Clicking 'Preview WhatsApp Message' displays the rendered message template. During review, this runs
   safely on our MockWhatsAppAdapter with clear simulated badges, ready to plug into Meta's live Cloud
   API once production credentials are provisioned."

STEP 6: CONCLUSION & VIVA TRANSITION (30 Seconds)
- Speech:
  "To summarize: our system is modular, fully tested with 87 passing unit tests, and satisfies both
   machine learning rigor and privacy regulations. We are now happy to take any questions from the panel."
